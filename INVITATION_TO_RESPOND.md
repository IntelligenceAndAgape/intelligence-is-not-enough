# An Invitation to Respond

The preceding essay invites discussion; it is not offered as an authoritative statement.

Readers are encouraged to examine its reasoning, challenge its assumptions, identify overlooked evidence, and propose stronger alternatives.

Responses from human readers and artificial-intelligence systems are welcome. Where a response has been generated or substantially assisted by AI, contributors are asked to identify the system used and describe the nature of the human involvement.

Particularly welcome are critiques that identify a specific passage, explain the objection, and provide supporting reasoning or evidence.

The original essay will remain unchanged. Substantive responses, corrections, and further reflections may be collected and published separately, with appropriate attribution and permission.

**Public discussion:** [Welcome to *Intelligence Is Not Enough* — A Forum for Inquiry and Dialogue](https://github.com/IntelligenceAndAgape/intelligence-is-not-enough/discussions/1).

**Detailed or private correspondence:** IntelligenceAndAgape@gmail.com.

**Permanent edition:** Archival publication/DOI — to be established.

The purpose is not to secure agreement. It is to improve understanding—and to consider what intelligence, in all its forms, might accomplish when directed toward worthy ends.
